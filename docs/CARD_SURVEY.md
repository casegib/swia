# Card Survey — deployment, items, agendas, heroes, companions, forms

Every non-mission card family in `lvisintini/imperial-assault-data` (MIT-licensed metadata + scans; card content © LFL/FFG), transcribed at 2× against an icon legend and then verified card-by-card in a second independent read. Command cards (skirmish) are excluded by decision. Source of truth is `docs/cards/*.json`; this file is generated by `scripts/build/card-survey.py`. Class cards are in `docs/class-cards.json`.

Notation: ⚡ surge · ✦ strain · ⊠ damage · [Block] · [Evade] · [Dodge] · ◆ threat · ⟶ action · [melee] / [ranged] · [Str]/[Ins]/[Tech] · [damage token] etc.

Second-pass verification corrected: deployment 5 of 161 (an affiliation, a keyword, a phantom ability, a Dodge/Block glyph), items 3 of 154 (formatting/name), agendas 38 of 114 (all formatting — a duplicated timing sentence and deck-name apostrophes; no rules changes), heroes 1 of 19 (Vinto's wounded Insight pool), companions and forms 0.
